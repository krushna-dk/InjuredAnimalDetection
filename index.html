TWO DEDICATED PIPELINES

How The System Works

Our platform operates through two dedicated reporting pipelines to ensure every injured animal is quickly identified and dispatched to rescue personnel.

Citizen Reporting

Module 1: Manual Report

Designed for citizens who spot an injured animal. Capture a live photo via device camera or upload an image, allow browser location access, select observed injury details, and submit. The report is recorded in MySQL and immediately forwarded to rescue teams.

Submit Manual Report
Vision AI Feed

Module 2: Automatic AI Detection

Connects to live camera streams (webcam or traffic/security cameras). Captures one frame every 5 seconds, sends it to the Gemini Vision AI for trauma assessment, applies a 10-minute duplicate prevention cooldown, and automatically generates rescue dispatches.
